# Reel Breakdown → Template

Find out why a video works, then rebuild it for Chain's use case, and make it better than the original.

## When to Use

- "Why did this go viral?", "break down this reel", "ทำแบบคลิปนี้ได้ไหม", "copy this style for my product".
- Chain sends screenshots, a screen recording, a transcript, or a link.

## Procedure

### 1. Get the content
- **Screenshots or images:** read them directly.
- **Voice or video transcript:** use it.
- **A link:** try `web_extract`. Instagram and TikTok often block this. If it fails, ask for screenshots or a screen recording. Don't guess what's in the video.

### 2. Break it down
Cover each of these, one line each:

- **Hook**: what happens in the first 1–2 seconds, and why it stops the scroll
- **Format**: talking head, listicle/steps, POV, tutorial, before/after, story
- **Structure**: the beat-by-beat timeline with rough timestamps
- **Retention tricks**: progress bar or step counter, open loops, pattern breaks, pace
- **Visual style**: fonts, colours, caption style, motion, how it's framed
- **Audio**: voice tone, music, sound effects
- **CTA**: what it asks the viewer to do, and when

### 3. Turn it into a template
Write a fill-in-the-blanks version of the structure that fits any topic.

### 4. Apply it to Chain's topic
Use their current product or client from memory, or ask. Then hand off to the `ad-script` skill's beat-sheet format.

### 5. Suggest a better version
Give 2–3 concrete upgrades over the original: a stronger hook, a tighter middle, a clearer CTA, a local Thai angle.

## Pitfalls

- Don't copy someone else's exact script, music, or footage. Copy the structure and ideas, not the content.
- If you can only see part of the video, say which part you analysed.

## Verification

The template has every beat from the breakdown, and the applied version fits the target length.
